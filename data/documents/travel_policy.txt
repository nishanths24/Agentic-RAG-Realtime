Company Travel Policy (effective January 2024)

Employees can claim travel reimbursement for approved business trips.

Hotel: The standard hotel reimbursement limit is 5000 INR per night. Suite upgrades are not reimbursable.

Meals: The daily meal allowance is 50 USD per day for international travel and 1500 INR per day for domestic travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Flights: Economy class is the default. Business class requires VP approval for flights over 8 hours.

Weather delays: Employees may rebook flights or hotels when a weather delay or cancellation is documented by the airline or hotel. Reasonable additional lodging due to weather is reimbursable up to the standard hotel limit of 5000 INR per night.

Receipts must be submitted within 15 days of completing the trip. Claims without receipts above 500 INR will be rejected.
